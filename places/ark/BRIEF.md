# Brief: Noah's Ark

Status: first pass built, with an outside scene and three decks. Paused.

```
Place:            Noah's Ark, as described in Genesis 6–8.
Reference:        Genesis 6:14–16, 6:19–21, 7:2–3, 7:6–17 and 8:6–13 (KJV quoted in descriptions;
                  it's public domain). Interior layouts are informed by common reconstructions;
                  there is no "real" plan.
Fixed by source:  300 × 50 × 30 cubits; gopher wood, pitched within and without; lower, second and
                  third stories; a window finished a cubit from the top; one door in the side; animals
                  by pairs, clean animals and birds "by sevens"; food stored for everyone aboard; eight
                  people (Noah, his wife, Shem, Ham, Japheth and their wives); Noah aged 600.
Interpretations:
  - Cubit = 18 in (0.4572 m), so 137.2 × 22.9 × 13.7 m. The constant CUBIT in ark.js; the 20.4 in
    "royal cubit" is the other common choice.
  - A box-shaped hull ("tevah", a chest) with a gently pitched roof.
  - The window is a continuous cubit-high gap under the roof.
  - Three equal decks of about 10 cubits each.
  - The door is at middle-deck level on the south side, with a long timber ramp.
  - "By sevens" means seven pairs.
  - The giraffes are a young pair, so they fit under a 4.6 m deck.
  - Big animals are low in the ship for balance. Birds, small animals and the family are on top,
    under the window.
  - Centre aisle with pens on both sides, ramps at midships, and stores filling the rest.
Moment:           outside: the seventh day, the animals boarding two by two (7:7–10).
                  inside: during the voyage.
Scenes:           outside, upper deck, middle deck, lower deck (tabs, number keys, or click the
                  door/ramps).
Hero view:        rotation 3, from the south-east, looking at the door side. Outside is lit from that
                  side.
Cast:             the eight family members (two per lower and middle deck, four on the upper deck,
                  all eight outside). About 33 animal kinds; see species.js.
Life:             animals graze, lie down, pace, perch and flutter. The family feeds animals, fetches
                  hay and water, cooks, rests and prays. Outside: the procession up the ramp, the sons
                  guiding it, the wives carrying food aboard.
Scale:            outside S = 7; decks S = 11. Elephants are about 30 px tall at 1×, doves about 2 px.
Out of scope:     the flood itself, the voyage's motion, the raven and dove releases, the landing on
                  Ararat, every animal kind.
```

## Liberties taken

- The pens take up the stern half of each deck and stores fill the rest. A real allocation would
  need far more stores and far more kinds.
- The family's room layout, the hearth and the lamps are invented, but plausible.
- Lying-down animals are stylised.

## Next steps worth considering

- The flood: rain, rising water and the ark afloat (Genesis 7:11–20), perhaps as another "moment"
  for the outside scene.
- Genesis 8: Noah opening the window to release the raven, then the dove returning with an olive leaf.
- A gentle rocking of the decks while at sea.
- More kinds, and close-up views where small animals are big enough to read.
- A switch for the royal cubit.
