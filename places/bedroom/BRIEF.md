# Brief: Van Gogh's Bedroom in Arles

Status: first pass.

```
Place:            Vincent van Gogh's bedroom on the first floor of the Yellow House, 2 Place Lamartine,
                  Arles, as painted in "The Bedroom" (October 1888; replicas in September 1889).
Reference:        The three paintings (Van Gogh Museum, Art Institute of Chicago, Musée d'Orsay; all
                  public domain). Van Gogh's letter to Theo of 16 October 1888 (letter 705), which
                  lists the colours. Wikipedia, "Bedroom in Arles", for the room's shape and doors.
Fixed by source:  trapezoid room (obtuse angle at the left corner of the window wall, acute at the
                  right); window in the front wall onto Place Lamartine; right door to the stairs,
                  left door to the guest room Gauguin used, so he walked through this room; bed
                  against the right wall; two rush chairs; a small toilet table with a jug and basin;
                  a mirror, towel and clothes on pegs; small portraits above the bed.
                  Colours (letter): pale violet walls, red tiles, butter-yellow bed and chairs,
                  lemon-green sheets and pillows, scarlet blanket, green window, orange table, blue
                  basin, lilac doors.
Interpretations:  No measured plan survives (the house was destroyed in 1944). Reported figures
                  disagree: "about 15 m² (160 sq ft)" and "about 12 × 17 ft" (19 m²). Used here: 12 ft
                  (3.66 m) wide, 3.8 m deep on the left and 4.6 m on the right, so about 15.4 m², with
                  a 3 m ceiling.
                  Exact placement follows the 1888 painting by eye.
Moment:           a day in late October 1888, compressed into a few minutes: dawn to night.
Scenes:           one room, shown with two palettes: "as described" (the letter) and "as it looks
                  today" (the faded pigments: walls blue, floor brownish).
Hero view:        rotation 2, from the doorway corner, so the window wall and the bed wall are full
                  height, like the painting. Turn the view to see the mirror and the left door.
Cast:             Vincent (ginger hair and beard, blue smock, straw hat when he goes out, pipe).
                  Paul Gauguin passes through in the evenings (he arrived on 23 October 1888).
Life:             sleeping, waking, washing at the basin, looking out of the window with a pipe,
                  leaving to paint, writing to Theo, napping. Gauguin comes home through the room.
                  The light moves from morning sun to candlelight to night.
Scale:            S = 90. The room is about 640 px across, and Vincent about 130 px tall at 1×.
Look:             painterly: short brushstroke textures and dark contour lines, as in the painting.
Out of scope:     the rest of the Yellow House, the studio, Place Lamartine outside.
```
