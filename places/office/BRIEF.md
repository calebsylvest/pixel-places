# Brief: Dunder Mifflin, Scranton

Status: first pass built, then rebuilt from the floorplan. Paused.

```
Place:            The Dunder Mifflin Scranton branch office from the TV series The Office.
Reference:        dunder-mifflin.png, a fan-drawn floorplan kept locally (not in the repo). It shows
                  the late seasons: Erin at reception, Gabe in the annex. Traced at about 50 plan px per metre, giving 24.9 × 12.7 m plus the
                  elevator lobby.
Fixed by source:  room layout (Michael's office, conference room, bullpen clusters, accounting nook,
                  Darryl's glass office, stairwell, kitchen, restrooms, Ryan's closet, annex, break
                  room), desk positions and who sits where, door positions.
Interpretations:  wall heights (2.9 m), furniture styling, glass walls and blinds, the stairwell drawn
                  as a pit to the warehouse, restrooms simplified, the bottom wall straightened.
Moment:           an ordinary weekday, around season 7. The cast follows the plan's labels.
Scenes:           one (the floor). The warehouse is off-model: people "go downstairs" and disappear
                  for a while.
Hero view:        rotation 3, looking from the south-east. The west and north walls are full height;
                  the sign is on the west wall behind reception.
Cast:             Michael, Dwight, Jim, Pam, Erin, Ryan, Andy, Stanley, Phyllis, Oscar, Angela, Kevin,
                  Meredith, Creed, Kelly, Toby, Gabe, Darryl, and a documentary camera operator.
Life:             desk work and phone calls, visiting each other, copier, water cooler, kitchen, vending,
                  restroom, elevator, warehouse. Timed events: Michael's conference-room meetings and
                  talking-head interviews filmed by the crew.
Scale:            S = 24. People are about 40 px tall at 1× zoom.
Out of scope:     the warehouse floor, sound, other seasons' layouts.
```

## Liberties taken

- Plants, a credenza and cabinets were moved or removed so every door stays passable (see the README
  on walkability).
- The Darryl and conference-room glass has no blinds, so it doesn't hide the desks behind it.
- Character descriptions are written fresh, not quoted from the show.
