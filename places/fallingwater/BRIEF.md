# Brief: Fallingwater

Status: first pass built.

```
Place:            Fallingwater, the Kaufmann house over the falls of Bear Run, Mill Run (Stewart
                  Township), Fayette County, Pennsylvania. Frank Lloyd Wright, designed 1935, built
                  1936–37; guest house 1939.
Reference:        HABS PA-5346 measured drawings (Kent State University, 2010 Peterson Prize entry),
                  Library of Congress, public domain; sheets 1–11 are in references/.
                  Sheet 2 = site plan; 3–6 = basement, first, second and third floor plans (they share
                  one registration, so one trace covers all floors); 7–8 = elevations; 9–10 = sections;
                  11 = the guest house.
                  Scale from the first floor's 98'-0" dimension: about 0.0525 m per sheet pixel
                  (1024 px wide JPEGs).
                  Levels from the sections: main floor 0'-0"; second terrace 9'-4"; third terrace
                  17'-9 3/4"; roof 24'-11"; main tower 32'-9"; hatch platform -7'-2".
Fixed by source:  the three floor plans (rooms, stone walls, window walls, terraces); the chimney stack;
                  the hatch and its stair to the stream; the four bolsters on the ledge; the trellis over
                  the drive bending round a tree; the bridge over Bear Run; the bridge from the second
                  floor to the hillside and the stair to the covered walk.
Interpretations:  the terrain is a stylised height field fitted to the plans' stream banks, falls lip
                  and hillside line; the falls' height (about 4 m here) and the slope of the woods are
                  by eye. Wall heights are storey heights from the sections; window mullions are evenly
                  spaced. Doors placed by eye (and two widened so everyone can walk through).
                  The plunge pool and the basement rooms are left out.
Moment:           a summer weekend around 1940, rhododendron in bloom, Wright visiting.
Scenes:           outside (the site, the house, Bear Run and the falls) and each of the three floors
                  cut away.
Hero view:        rotation 2, from the south-west, downstream: the classic view up at the terraces
                  over the falls.
Cast:             Edgar J. Kaufmann, Liliane Kaufmann, Edgar Kaufmann jr., Frank Lloyd Wright, a weekend
                  guest, and household staff (cook, housemaid, caretaker) as unnamed roles.
Life:             one timetable for everyone, shared by all four scenes: morning in their rooms;
                  breakfast and dinner (the timed event, at seven) at the table at the north end of the
                  living room; the afternoon swim in Bear Run down the hatch stair; reading by the fire,
                  sketching the house from below the falls, the caretaker on the drive and bridge.
Scale:            outside S = 11; main floor S = 22; second floor S = 16; third floor S = 26.
Engine upgrades:  none needed. New in this Place: a height-field terrain with water levels above and
                  below the falls, a waterfall animated in the overlay, walls placed directly (stone,
                  glass, parapets) instead of rasterPlan, and a cutaway that keeps only far outside walls
                  at full height.
Out of scope:     the guest house and the upper part of the covered walk, the plunge pool, the
                  basement, the road.
```
